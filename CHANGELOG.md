# Changelog

## 2.5.0
- First public release.
- Source split into modules under `src/`, built into a single userscript with `node build.mjs`.

## 2.4.x
- Clear all pins and marks in a conversation, with confirmation and undo.
- Expanded pins keep their title row fixed while scrolling.
- Separate resize handle and scrollbar in the side-docked panel.

## 2.0 – 2.3
- Full-conversation loading so long chats can be navigated.
- Automatic titles from the question, or from the answer when the question is vague.
- Pinned panel docked at the top or on the left, resizable.
- Saved prompts beside the message box.
